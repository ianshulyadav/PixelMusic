PixelMusic FAQ

What is PixelMusic (Pixel Music)?

PixelMusic, also searched as Pixel Music, is a free and open-source Android music player. It plays local files and streams from YouTube Music and Telegram, and it is built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive.

Is PixelMusic really free and ad-free?

Yes. There are no ads anywhere in the app. PixelMusic is free to use and open source under the GNU GPL v3.0.

Which Android versions does it support?

Android 11 (API 30) and above.

Which audio formats can it play?

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, APE, AAC, MP3, OGG, OPUS, and Dolby Digital, Digital Plus and Atmos (E-AC-3 JOC). Dolby playback depends on the decoders in your device.

Can I stream YouTube Music?

Yes. Sign in to YouTube Music inside the app, then search, stream and sync your liked songs, playlists and artists. This integration is unofficial and meant for personal use with content you are allowed to access.

Can I play music from Telegram?

Yes. PixelMusic can stream audio from your Telegram channels, chats and saved messages.

Where do the lyrics come from?

From LRCLIB. You can fix timing with an offset, translate lines, and see romanization. Lyrics are saved for offline use.

How do I install PixelMusic?

Download it from the PixelMusicApp repository on GitHub, open the file, and allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks. Then open the app and allow access to your music files.

Is PixelMusic affiliated with Google, YouTube or Telegram?

No. PixelMusic is an independent, unofficial fork of PixelPlayerOSS and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube Music, Telegram or their parent companies.

How can I support PixelMusic?

Use the Ko-fi or UPI options in the Support section below. Sharing the app and sending feedback also helps.
